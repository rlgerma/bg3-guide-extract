PHASE 1 — Rivington: complete the approach to the city

Starting position: Rivington and Sharess’ Caress waypoints are already known. Backtrack here to audit activities, not to replay completed scenes. Default party, except Gale for Tara and Astarion for Gur.

1A. Refugees, Strange Ox and explosive toys

  • R01 — Walk the refugee camp and Rivington merchants. Talk to returning tieflings, Yenna, refugees, guards and Rivington General. Check survivors’ unique conversations and buy supplies. CHECK IN GAME: which of these have already happened.

Carry it into the Lower City for the Ox as a final ally. Multiplayer special: the apple should be held by the multiplayer host (Chad, if he is hosting) or a companion when entering the Lower City; the wiki documents missing rewards when carried by a non-host player avatar.

  • C26 — Go to Lady Jannath’s Estate in the western Lower City. Find Oskar Fevras, possessed inside a haunted house. Climb carefully through poltergeists and cursed skulls; avoid killing Oskar.

PHASE 5 — Optional high-stakes dungeons before the main villains

5A. House of Hope: Orphic Hammer, Raphael, and the best monk gloves

Party: Chad + Maurow + Gale + Lae’zel. This is a major level-11/12 encounter, especially with extra-enemy mods. Complete at a point when you can buy consumables and long rest freely. Save before entering and before taking the central Archive item.

  • H01 — Get into the House of Hope through Helsik’s Devil’s Fee (perform/acquire the ritual to open the portal). Before entry, secure fire resistance, healing, Counterspell, high-damage abilities and control. Talk to Helsik about the Orphic Hammer.

  • H03 — Loot the Archive pedestals: Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength (sets STR to 23), Amulet of Greater Health (sets CON to 23) and the central Orphic Hammer if you rejected Raphael, or Soul-Sworn Contract if you signed. Both side pedestals have traps/alarms; arrange escape plan first.
  • H04 — Get the Archive passphrase via the Boudoir/story interactions and remove the shield on the Hammer/contract. Pick up Mol’s contract if her quest is available; record whether you intend to return it.
  • H05 — Thoroughly loot House of Hope special gear such as the Helldusk Helmet, other hidden valuables and any loot from Haarlep (including Helldusk Gloves if you fight and defeat him). Do not assume mutually exclusive romance/combat paths can both occur.
  • H06 — Free Hope. Equip the Orphic Hammer, go to her prison, survive Spectators/imps and break both binding crystals with Unshackling Strike. Hope becomes an attached follower; keep her alive.
  • H07 — Beat Raphael in the entrance hall. Plan for Soul Pillars, Raphael’s magic and Yurgir’s involvement; Counterspell, focused pillar damage and control are valuable. Persuade Yurgir to help if the dialogue and prior-game state permit. Protect Hope throughout.
  • H08 — Collect Raphael’s gear including Helldusk Armour and all appropriate boss loot; obtain Gloves of Soul Catching from surviving Hope. Maurow: these are an exceptional upgrade for an Open Hand Monk. Return safely to the Devil’s Fee.
  • H09 — Return to Voss with the Orphic Hammer (if applicable), get Silver Sword of the Astral Plane, and progress Free Orpheus. Do not use the Hammer to make the final Orpheus choice until the final Astral Prism sequence.

5B. Wyll’s Wyrmway and Ansur

Party: Chad + Maurow + Wyll + Gale or Lae’zel. Wyll may receive his Legend of Ansur clue after the Duke rescue or through Florrick/Cordula Eltan depending on earlier choices. He is only Lv 4 in the save: level up at camp first or use Gale/Lae’zel for difficult fight if the companion dialogue is already complete.

  • H16 — Enter Ansur’s sanctum. Read the environmental lore, then interact with the ancient dragon for the revelations and battle. Prepare lightning resistance, spread out before massive AoE, and kill the revived dragon.
  • H17 — Loot Balduran’s Giantslayer and Helm of Balduran; speak with Wyll and Duke Ravengard (if alive) to resolve Wyll’s future as Blade of Avernus versus political path. The Giantslayer is an excellent candidate for Chad if his current setup uses a two-handed weapon.

PHASE 6 — Iron Throne → Steel Watch Foundry → Gortash

Order is mandatory for the fullest rescue route. Take plenty of mobility and healing consumables; mods may change encounter counts or timing. The save currently has PLA_GrandDukeRescue and WYR_GetGortashGem active.

6A. The Iron Throne: rescue the hostages before sabotage

Party: Chad + Maurow + Gale + Lae’zel, or replace Lae’zel with Wyll for father/son significance. Ensure Gale has Haste, Misty Step or comparable mobility, Counterspell and possibly Dimension Door or scrolls. Maurow’s Step of the Wind makes him particularly good at traversing distant cell blocks.

  • G01 — Verify Wyll’s Mizora scene is complete and Gortash’s coronation was attended. Otherwise Duke Ravengard can end up in an unexpected/dead state. Record Wyll’s pact choice, then inspect your journal for Duke clues.
  • G02 — Begin Avenge the Drowned at the Water Queen’s House if available. Speak with Allandra Grey about the drowned servant and track the submersible operator. This quest crosses directly with Iron Throne access; do it before the rescue for full dialogue.
  • G03 — Explore Flymm Cargo at the docks. Defeat/avoid the warehouse guards, find the hidden submarine dock and meet Redhammer. Learn about Iron Throne and its captives. Negotiate to reach the prison, rather than killing him before hearing the story.
  • G04 — Make a dedicated hard save before taking the submarine. The mission becomes a timed extraction after arrival. On the medium/default ruleset the vanilla Iron Throne gives six turns (modded difficulty can change this). Gortash threatens the prison; proceed with rescue if this is your intended branch.
  • G05 — Split the party into rescue lanes immediately. Maurow: sprint/Step of the Wind to far cages and open levers. Chad: fight/block Sahuagin and open another wing. Gale: Haste/teleport, emergency prisoner movement and crowd control. Lae’zel/Wyll: rescue Duke/nearby hostages and help secure the ladder. Interact with each cell lever; it usually saves more actions than killing everything.
  • G06 — Rescue Duke Ulder Ravengard. If Wyll broke his pact, watch for Mizora’s intervention/spiders and immediately heal or protect the Duke. Get him to the ladder/submersible alive before the timer expires.
  • G07 — Free all available Gondian prisoners, particularly Obelia Toobin (Zanner’s daughter), because their survival affects the Foundry rebellion. A perfect rescue may need a rehearsed route and a reload; do not waste turns opening loot containers.
  • G08 — Rescue Omeluum if present. It is held in a southwest separate room. Teleport to Submersible can take Omeluum and one ally back, making a distant rescue viable. Its presence depends on earlier-game events.
  • G09 — Board the submarine with as many rescued NPCs as possible before the last turn; check the party and prisoner counts in the mission summary. Back on land, resolve Redhammer versus the Waveservants in Avenge the Drowned as consciously chosen (the Wavemother’s Robe is linked to their side of the conflict).
  • G10 — Talk to Duke Ravengard, Wyll, Mizora and rescued NPCs at camp/appropriate locations. Confirm Rescue the Grand Duke, Retrieve Omeluum and the hostage aspect of Save the Gondians where available.

6B. Steel Watch Foundry: save the workers, then destroy production

Party: Default; if a story companion is desired, bring Wyll or Karlach only after raising levels. Be alert for Banite Motivators: deactivate them fast or the collared Gondians die.

  • G11 — Enter the Steel Watch Foundry in the Grey Harbour / southwest Lower City. Talk to Zanner Toobin; with his daughter rescued he can help dismantle the operation. If you accidentally killed Gortash first, these interactions may be gone.
  • G12 — Save Gondians on the production floor. Defeat Banites while keeping workers alive and deactivate dropped Motivators immediately. Watch AoE targeting around Gondian allies.

  • G14 — Descend to the Control Centre with Zanner (or the relevant code/runepowder alternative) and defeat the Steel Watch Titan and guards, unless you intentionally take a bypass. Inspect the Titan for Gontr Mael if the boss is defeated and lootable.

6C. Gortash: after the hostages and Foundry

Party: Chad + Maurow + Karlach + Gale for narrative, or Gale + Lae’zel for the default strongest frontline. Karlach is Lv 4 in this save; level her up before using her.

  • G17 — Return to Wyrm’s Rock after disabling the Steel Watch. Expect hostile security and remaining traps. Work through the fortress methodically and clear dangerous mechanisms where possible.
  • G18 — Confront and defeat Enver Gortash. Use Counterspell, disables, throws/positioning, and concentrate fire on the boss while respecting trap/environmental hazards. Loot the Netherstone and all unique items.
  • G19 — Bring Karlach for the immediate post-Gortash scene and follow-up. Her personal resolution is worth the swap even if you used the default party to defeat Gortash; check the journal, camp and Dammon if applicable.
  • G20 — Resolve Wyll/Duke aftermath and check for reactions from other allies, including Florrick. Verify no unfinished Gortash-related quest remains that is still achievable.

PHASE 7 — Murder Tribunal, Orin, and the Undercity
